# Research direction

AIcontrol helps a person get more out of a language model on the question they
came to investigate. The original brief is their investment. The Research tab
should make their inquiry deeper, more precise and easier to resume.

## How the research should feel

- **The map holds the work.** Save the hypotheses, mechanisms, comparisons,
  alternatives, assumptions and possible tests the model chooses to present.
  Preserve the branch path and authorship so someone can see how an idea
  follows from earlier work. Researcher notes record why a person kept an idea.
  Treat model contributions as provisional until there is evidence.
- **The chatbot speaks selectively.** Answer the user's immediate question in
  plain language. Bring up an idea from the map when it changes the explanation,
  challenges an assumption, or offers a better way to test the idea. Use a small
  amount of wider context when it helps the selected branch. The map saves
  research outputs that can be shared with the user.
- **The user steers the investigation.** Growth starts with the original brief
  and the selected branch. Keep digging while the branch has useful depth. A
  new goal needs an explicit choice. Automatic growth can stop when the model
  finds no worthwhile extension.
- **Possible questions are invitations.** Put the current suggestion at the
  bottom of its branch in the left list, after its saved descendants. Let it
  evolve when the branch gains something material. Draw mainly on the branch,
  with the wider map as a secondary comparison. The chatbot can mention it at
  the right moment. A suggestion alone gives the system no reason to change
  direction.
- **Related ideas can sit together.** Use meaning to arrange substantive ideas.
  Keep the original branch and breadcrumbs available. Record the kind of
  contribution and specific topic, mechanism, outcome and condition tags where
  useful. Two ideas can be similar in subject and disagree in their claims.
  Show unusual ideas at the edge as candidates for exploration. Position
  expresses relatedness. Value and credibility require separate judgment.
- **Evidence comes when the user chooses it.** Let the model develop and refine
  the inquiry first. Evidence is a deliberate later step for a selected branch.
  Keep a clear distinction between an idea, a suggested test, a retrieved
  source and what that source actually supports.

## Before a change that shifts this direction

Use this document to review Research changes. A substantial departure includes
automatic topic changes, frequent questions that interrupt the conversation,
similarity changing branch ownership or steering, evidence-first defaults, or
changes that take control of the inquiry away from the user.

For a substantial departure, tell the project owner what would change, why and
how it would affect the experience. Get their agreement before implementing
that departure. Routine improvements consistent with this direction follow the
normal development and release workflow.
